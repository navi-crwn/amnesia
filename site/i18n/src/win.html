03 · Sign out

Sign out. Forgotten.Sign out. Terlupakan.

Sign out, restart or shut down and Amnesia wipes all of it. Amnesia starts cleaning the moment Windows asks "may I sign out?", and Windows shows "Amnesia is cleaning up..." until it's done. Only what you chose stays: your Keep folder, your Keep List and the logins in your vault. Your installed programs stay too.Begitu kamu sign out, restart, atau shut down, Amnesia menghapus semua itu. Amnesia mulai membersihkan tepat saat Windows bertanya "boleh sign out?", dan Windows menampilkan "Amnesia is cleaning up..." sampai selesai. Yang tersisa hanya pilihanmu: folder Keep, Keep List, dan login di vault. Program yang sudah terpasang juga tetap ada.

At the next sign in it checks once more, so a crash or a power cut doesn't leave traces behind.Saat sign in berikutnya, Amnesia mengecek sekali lagi. Jadi crash atau mati listrik pun tidak meninggalkan jejak.

Selective Amnesia is the name for this: everything is forgotten, except what you chose to keep.artinya: semua dilupakan, kecuali yang kamu pilih untuk disimpan.

    05 · Made for a real PCUntuk PC sungguhan

    Wipes what it should. Leaves what it shouldn't.Menghapus yang perlu. Membiarkan yang jangan.

    Version 0.8 came from a full audit and a test on a real Windows 11 laptop. These are the rules it follows at every sign out.Versi 0.8 lahir dari audit lengkap dan tes di laptop Windows 11 sungguhan. Ini aturan yang diikuti setiap sign out.

    • Cleans the moment Windows asks.Membersihkan begitu Windows bertanya. So a normal sign out is clean before the sign in screen shows. Files Windows still holds open go at the next sign in, and the safe box closes after the cleanup.Jadi sign out biasa sudah bersih sebelum layar sign in muncul. File yang masih dipegang Windows dihapus saat sign in berikutnya, dan brankas ditutup sesudah pembersihan.
    • Cloud folders stay.Folder cloud tetap ada. Moving them away would look like "deleted" to the sync app, and it could delete your files in the cloud too. Files that only live in the cloud are left alone as well.Kalau dipindah, app sync akan menganggapnya "dihapus" dan bisa ikut menghapus file kamu di cloud. File yang cuma ada di cloud juga tidak disentuh.
    • Virtual machines stay.Mesin virtual tetap ada. VM disks and disc images of 1 GB or more are kept. You can switch this off in Settings, Protection.Disk VM dan disc image 1 GB ke atas disimpan. Bisa dimatikan di Pengaturan, Perlindungan.
    • New apps wait 3 days.App baru menunggu 3 hari. With "Remove new apps" on, a new app's folder goes to Amnesia's trash first, never straight to nothing. Press Keep and its program and data folders go on your Keep List.Kalau "Hapus app baru" nyala, folder app baru masuk tempat sampah Amnesia dulu, tidak langsung hilang. Tekan Simpan dan folder program serta datanya masuk Keep List.

    Honest trade-off: because the cleanup starts when Windows asks, cancelling the sign out after that point doesn't bring anything back. It's like pressing "Clean now".Konsekuensi jujur: karena pembersihan mulai saat Windows bertanya, membatalkan sign out setelah itu tidak mengembalikan apa pun. Sama seperti menekan "Bersihkan sekarang".

    06 · Profile Vault

    One password. Your logins back.Satu password. Login kembali.

    An encrypted safe for your app logins: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, Session, Element, Obsidian, Thunderbird, Bitwarden, VS Code, Claude, Antigravity, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Git and any folder you add. Sign in to a clean PC, type one password, and they're back.Brankas terenkripsi untuk login app kamu: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, Session, Element, Obsidian, Thunderbird, Bitwarden, VS Code, Claude, Antigravity, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Git, dan folder apa pun yang kamu tambahkan. Sign in ke PC yang bersih, ketik satu password, dan semuanya kembali.

    • AES-256. Every snapshot gets its own random key, locked with the vault's key. Forget the password and nobody can open it.Setiap snapshot punya kunci acak sendiri, dikunci dengan kunci vault. Kalau lupa password, tidak ada yang bisa membukanya.
    • Saved after you close each app.Disimpan setelah app ditutup. In the background, only when something changed. Sign out just cleans, so it's quick.Di latar belakang, hanya kalau ada yang berubah. Sign out cuma membersihkan, jadi cepat.
    • A spare snapshot.Snapshot cadangan. Each app keeps the one before the newest, in case the newest looks wrong.Setiap app menyimpan snapshot sebelum yang terbaru, kalau-kalau yang terbaru terlihat salah.
    • Finds your apps.Menemukan app kamu. It suggests installed apps it doesn't know yet, and a meter shows how hard your password is to guess.Vault menyarankan app terpasang yang belum dikenalnya, dan ada meteran yang menunjukkan seberapa sulit password kamu ditebak.

    Memory Recall Restore brings your logins back with your password. A notification after sign in reminds you.Pulihkan mengembalikan login kamu dengan password. Notifikasi setelah sign in mengingatkan kamu.

    Global Amnesia 3 wrong passwords or your panic word, and the vault is gone for good.Salah password 3 kali atau mengetik kata panik, dan vault hilang selamanya.

    Honest note: Windows ties saved logins to your Windows user. Snapshots come back on this PC and this user, not on another PC.Catatan jujur: Windows mengikat login tersimpan ke user Windows kamu. Snapshot kembali di PC ini dan user ini, bukan di PC lain.

    08 · Backup

    Backup, and a new PC.Backup, dan PC baru.

    09 · Quick exitsKeluar cepat

    Need to go? It cleans up.Harus pergi? Langsung bersih.

    For nosy people, a borrowed PC, or someone pushing you to "just open it". All of these are off until you turn them on in Settings.Untuk orang yang kepo, PC yang dipinjam, atau orang yang memaksa "buka sebentar saja". Semua ini mati sampai kamu nyalakan di Pengaturan.

    Panic hotkeyTombol panik

    Press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + X (or Q, or F12). The screen locks at once, Amnesia cleans up and signs you out without waiting for other apps. It works while paused too, and tries again if Windows is slow.Tekan Ctrl + Alt + Shift + X (atau Q, atau F12). Layar langsung terkunci, Amnesia membersihkan dan sign out tanpa menunggu app lain. Tetap jalan saat dijeda, dan mencoba lagi kalau Windows lambat.

    CtrlAltShiftX

    USB keyFlashdisk kunci

    Pick a USB drive. Pull it out and Amnesia cleans and signs out, or cleans and shuts down. Your choice.Pilih satu flashdisk. Cabut, dan Amnesia membersihkan lalu sign out, atau membersihkan lalu mematikan PC. Kamu yang pilih.

    Sign out when idleSign out saat ditinggal

    Walked away? After 5 to 60 minutes you get a 60 second countdown, then it locks, cleans and signs out. Move the mouse to stop it.Lupa ditinggal? Setelah 5 sampai 60 menit muncul hitung mundur 60 detik, lalu layar dikunci, dibersihkan, dan sign out. Gerakkan mouse untuk membatalkan.

    Doomsday

    3 wrong vault passwords in a row delete the vault for good. Amnesia warns you before the last try. You can switch it off.Salah password vault 3 kali berturut-turut menghapus vault selamanya. Amnesia memperingatkan sebelum percobaan terakhir. Bisa dimatikan.

    Honest fine print: Amnesia protects you after a sign out. While you're signed in, your PC is as open as always, so lock it (Win+L). Quick exits don't wait for unsaved work, and the panic word can't be undone.Catatan jujur: Amnesia melindungi kamu setelah sign out.     10 · LocksKunci

    Lock it down, honestly.Kunci rapat, dengan jujur.

    Two locks in Settings, Advanced, and a safe box in your Keep List page. Here's what they do, and what they don't.Dua kunci di Pengaturan, Lanjutan, dan brankas di halaman Keep List. Ini yang bisa, dan yang tidak bisa mereka lakukan.

    Keep lockKunci Keep

    Your Keep folder only opens through Amnesia, with its own password: Open Keep, the tray menu or the Desktop shortcut. It locks again when you lock the screen (Win+L), after the minutes you pick, and at sign out and sign in. While locked, no program can open or write files in it.Folder Keep hanya bisa dibuka lewat Amnesia, dengan password sendiri: Buka Keep, menu tray, atau shortcut di Desktop. Terkunci lagi saat layar dikunci (Win+L), setelah menit yang kamu pilih, serta saat sign out dan sign in. Selama terkunci, tidak ada program yang bisa membuka atau menulis file di sana.

    • The files are not encrypted. Use BitLocker for that.File-nya tidak dienkripsi. Pakai BitLocker untuk itu.
    • File names stay visible while it's locked.Nama file tetap kelihatan saat terkunci.
    • Someone signed in as you who knows Windows can remove the lock in the folder's Properties.Orang yang sign in sebagai kamu dan paham Windows bisa melepas kuncinya lewat Properties folder.

    Lab lockKunci lab

    For a PC many people use, like a computer lab or a library. An administrator sets a PIN of 6 or more digits. From then on this Windows user can't turn Amnesia off, pause it, quit it, uninstall it or change its settings and Keep List. Amnesia starts at every sign in, and the PIN opens the settings for 10 minutes.Untuk PC yang dipakai banyak orang, seperti lab komputer atau perpustakaan. Administrator memasang PIN minimal 6 angka. Setelah itu user Windows ini tidak bisa mematikan, menjeda, menutup, meng-uninstall Amnesia, atau mengubah pengaturan dan Keep List. Amnesia selalu jalan setiap sign in, dan PIN membuka pengaturan selama 10 menit.

    • Someone can still end Amnesia in Task Manager. The check at the next sign in cleans anyway.Orang masih bisa menutup Amnesia lewat Task Manager. Pengecekan saat sign in berikutnya tetap membersihkan.
    • Set your settings and Keep List first. They're locked as they are.Atur pengaturan dan Keep List dulu. Semuanya terkunci apa adanya.

    Safe boxBrankas experimentaleksperimen

    A locked folder inside Keep for your most private files, with its own password. Closed, it is one encrypted file (AES-256-GCM, the same format as the vault). Open, it is Keep\Safe. Amnesia closes it again at every sign out, after the cleanup.Folder terkunci di dalam Keep untuk file paling pribadi, dengan password sendiri. Saat tertutup, isinya satu file terenkripsi (AES-256-GCM, format yang sama dengan vault). Saat dibuka, ada di Keep\Safe. Amnesia menutupnya lagi setiap sign out, sesudah pembersihan.

    • Keep it small: closing packs everything again, and that takes time.Isinya jangan terlalu besar: menutup berarti mengemas semuanya lagi, dan itu butuh waktu.
    • Forget its password and nobody can open it, you included.Kalau lupa password-nya, tidak ada yang bisa membukanya, termasuk kamu.
    • Uninstall warns you while it still has files in it.Uninstall memberi peringatan selama brankas masih berisi file.

        Nothing is deleted before step 7.Belum ada yang dihapus sebelum langkah 7.

        17 · FAQ

        Questions, answered.Pertanyaan, terjawab.

        SafetyKeamanan

        Can I try it without deleting anything?Bisa dicoba tanpa menghapus apa-apa?

        Yes. After you install it, Amnesia is Off and deletes nothing. Open What gets wiped and press Show preview: it only lists what would go. Turn it on when the list looks right.Bisa. Setelah dipasang, Amnesia dalam keadaan Mati dan tidak menghapus apa pun. Buka Yang dihapus dan tekan Lihat simulasi: isinya cuma daftar yang akan hilang. Nyalakan kalau daftarnya sudah pas.

        Where do wiped files go? Can I get them back?File yang dihapus pindah ke mana? Bisa dikembalikan?

        Nowhere you can reach. They don't go to the Recycle Bin and can't be brought back. Once Amnesia is on, it really deletes, so check What gets wiped first and put what you need in Keep or on the Keep List.Tidak ke mana pun yang bisa kamu buka. Tidak masuk Recycle Bin dan tidak bisa dikembalikan. Begitu Amnesia menyala, ia benar-benar menghapus, jadi cek Yang dihapus dulu dan simpan yang kamu perlukan di Keep atau Keep List.

        Is my Keep folder encrypted?Apakah folder Keep dienkripsi?

        No. Amnesia keeps it, it doesn't encrypt it. Turn on BitLocker or device encryption: Settings → Privacy & security → Device encryption. Amnesia's Settings shows whether it's on.Tidak. Amnesia menyimpannya, tapi tidak mengenkripsinya. Nyalakan BitLocker atau enkripsi perangkat: Settings → Privacy & security → Device encryption. Pengaturan Amnesia menunjukkan apakah sudah menyala.

        What if the PC crashes or loses power?Bagaimana kalau PC crash atau mati listrik?

        The check at the next sign in catches it and does the cleanup.Pengecekan saat sign in berikutnya akan menangkapnya dan membersihkan.

        What if I cancel a sign out?Bagaimana kalau sign out dibatalkan?

        Amnesia cleans the moment Windows asks "may I sign out?". If you cancel after that, for example because an app asked to save, the cleanup has already run. It's like pressing "Clean now". Use Review & sign out when you're not sure.Amnesia membersihkan begitu Windows bertanya "boleh sign out?". Kalau kamu batalkan setelah itu, misalnya karena ada app yang minta menyimpan, pembersihan sudah berjalan. Sama seperti menekan "Bersihkan sekarang". Pakai Cek & sign out kalau kamu ragu.

        Are OneDrive, Dropbox or my virtual machines wiped?Apakah OneDrive, Dropbox, atau mesin virtual saya ikut dihapus?

        No. Cloud folders (OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Nextcloud, MEGA, Box, pCloud, Proton Drive and any Windows sync folder) are left alone, because moving them could delete your files in the cloud too. VM disks and disc images of 1 GB or more stay as well, unless you switch that off in Settings, Protection.Tidak. Folder cloud (OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Nextcloud, MEGA, Box, pCloud, Proton Drive, dan folder sync Windows lainnya) dibiarkan, karena memindahkannya bisa ikut menghapus file kamu di cloud. Disk VM dan disc image 1 GB ke atas juga tetap ada, kecuali kamu matikan di Pengaturan, Perlindungan.

        How do I know it's working?Bagaimana saya tahu Amnesia jalan?

        Open Health check on the home page. Each line is green, yellow or red and says where to fix it. History shows every cleanup with how many items went.Buka Cek kesehatan di Beranda. Setiap baris hijau, kuning, atau merah, dan bilang di mana memperbaikinya. Riwayat menampilkan setiap pembersihan dengan jumlah item yang dihapus.

        Is my data sent anywhere?Apakah data saya dikirim ke suatu tempat?

        No. No servers, no accounts, no tracking. Backups go only where you pick. The update check asks GitHub for the newest version number (every week by default), and you can switch it off.Tidak. Tanpa server, tanpa akun, tanpa pelacakan. Backup hanya pergi ke tempat yang kamu pilih. Cek update menanyakan nomor versi terbaru ke GitHub (bawaannya setiap minggu), dan bisa dimatikan.

        Windows Security or my antivirus blocks it. Now what?Windows Security atau antivirus memblokirnya. Lalu?

        Home tells you when something was refused. Usually it's Controlled folder access: Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Ransomware protection → Allow an app, and add Amnesia. Please don't turn your antivirus off.Beranda memberi tahu kalau ada yang ditolak. Biasanya ini Controlled folder access: Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Ransomware protection → Allow an app, lalu tambahkan Amnesia. Jangan matikan antivirus kamu.

        Locks and quick exitsKunci dan keluar cepat

        Can someone get around the Keep lock?Bisakah kunci Keep diakali?

        Yes, someone signed in as you who knows Windows can remove it in the folder's Properties. The files aren't encrypted either. It stops casual snooping and other programs, not a determined person. For real protection, use BitLocker.Bisa, orang yang sign in sebagai kamu dan paham Windows bisa melepasnya lewat Properties folder. File-nya juga tidak dienkripsi. Kunci ini menahan orang iseng dan program lain, bukan orang yang benar-benar niat. Untuk perlindungan sungguhan, pakai BitLocker.

        I forgot my Keep lock password.Saya lupa password kunci Keep.

        Open the Keep folder's Properties → Security and remove the "Deny" lines. Your files are still there.Buka Properties folder Keep → Security, lalu hapus baris "Deny". File kamu masih ada.

        What happens with the panic hotkey?Apa yang terjadi dengan tombol panik?

        The screen locks right away, Amnesia cleans up and signs you out without waiting for other apps. Unsaved work is lost. The tray menu has the same thing: Panic sign out now.Layar langsung terkunci, Amnesia membersihkan dan sign out tanpa menunggu app lain. Pekerjaan yang belum disimpan hilang. Menu tray punya hal yang sama: Sign out darurat sekarang.

        What is the safe box?Apa itu brankas?

        An experimental locked folder inside Keep, for your most private files. Closed, it's one encrypted file (AES-256-GCM, like the vault). Open, it's Keep\Safe. Amnesia closes it again at every sign out, after the cleanup. Keep it small: closing packs everything again.Folder terkunci di dalam Keep yang masih eksperimen, untuk file paling pribadi. Saat tertutup, isinya satu file terenkripsi (AES-256-GCM, seperti vault). Saat terbuka, ada di Keep\Safe. Amnesia menutupnya lagi setiap sign out, sesudah pembersihan. Isinya jangan terlalu besar: menutup berarti mengemas semuanya lagi.

        Can the lab lock be stopped?Bisakah kunci lab dihentikan?

        Someone can end Amnesia in Task Manager. The check at the next sign in still cleans. Changing settings or turning it off needs the administrator's PIN.Orang bisa menutup Amnesia lewat Task Manager. Pengecekan saat sign in berikutnya tetap membersihkan. Mengubah pengaturan atau mematikannya butuh PIN administrator.

        Profile Vault

        What if I forget the vault password?Kalau lupa password vault?

        Then nobody can open it, including you. Make a new vault and sign in to your apps again. Write the password down somewhere safe.Tidak ada yang bisa membukanya, termasuk kamu. Buat vault baru, lalu login ulang ke app kamu. Catat password-nya di tempat aman.

        When is the vault saved?Kapan vault disimpan?

        After you close an app that changed, in the background (checked every minute). Tools without a window, like Claude Code or Git, after 3 minutes without changes. If Edge keeps running in the background ("startup boost"), it's saved once it really closes.Setelah kamu menutup app yang berubah, di latar belakang (dicek setiap menit). Alat tanpa jendela, seperti Claude Code atau Git, setelah 3 menit tanpa perubahan. Kalau Edge tetap jalan di latar belakang ("startup boost"), ia disimpan setelah benar-benar tertutup.

        Do my logins work on another PC?Apakah login saya jalan di PC lain?

        Usually not. Windows ties saved logins to your Windows user, so snapshots come back on this PC and this user. Move to a new PC brings your Keep folder, Keep List and vault, but you may need to sign in to some apps again.Biasanya tidak. Windows mengikat login tersimpan ke user Windows kamu, jadi snapshot kembali di PC ini dan user ini. Pindah ke PC baru membawa folder Keep, Keep List, dan vault, tapi kamu mungkin perlu login ulang ke beberapa app.

        The newest snapshot looks wrong.Snapshot terbaru terlihat salah.

        If a new snapshot is much smaller than the last one, the vault says so. Tick "Use the snapshot before the newest one" when you restore.Kalau snapshot baru jauh lebih kecil dari sebelumnya, vault memberi tahu. Centang "Pakai snapshot sebelum yang terbaru" saat memulihkan.

        What are the panic word and Doomsday?Apa itu kata panik dan Doomsday?

        The panic word is an optional word you pick. Typed in the password box, it deletes the vault (and your Keep folder too, if you chose so). Doomsday deletes the vault after 3 wrong passwords in a row. You can switch Doomsday off.Kata panik adalah kata opsional pilihanmu. Kalau diketik di kolom password, vault terhapus (folder Keep juga, kalau kamu memilihnya). Doomsday menghapus vault setelah 3 kali salah password berturut-turut. Doomsday bisa dimatikan.

        Install and updatesPasang dan update

        Why does Windows warn me when I install it?Kenapa Windows memberi peringatan saat dipasang?

        The app isn't signed yet, so Windows doesn't know it. Press More info, then Run anyway. Want to be sure? Compare the file with SHA256SUMS.txt from the same release.App-nya belum bertanda tangan, jadi Windows belum mengenalnya. Tekan More info, lalu Run anyway. Mau lebih yakin? Cocokkan file-nya dengan SHA256SUMS.txt dari rilis yang sama.

        x64 or arm64?x64 atau arm64?

        Most PCs need x64. Pick arm64 for Windows on ARM, like a Snapdragon laptop or a Windows virtual machine on a Mac.Kebanyakan PC butuh x64. Pilih arm64 untuk Windows ARM, seperti laptop Snapdragon atau mesin virtual Windows di Mac.

        Does it need admin?Perlu admin?

        No. It installs just for you. Only a few optional things ask Windows for admin once: Windows protections, deleting leftover folders and the lab lock.Tidak. Terpasang khusus untuk kamu. Hanya beberapa hal opsional yang minta izin admin sekali: perlindungan Windows, menghapus folder sisa app, dan kunci lab.

        How do I update?Bagaimana cara update?

        Settings → Update → Check now. Turn Amnesia off before you install the update, then turn it on again. Amnesia installs an update only when the developer's signature checks out; otherwise it opens the release page.Pengaturan → Update → Cek sekarang. Matikan Amnesia sebelum memasang update, lalu nyalakan lagi. Amnesia hanya memasang update kalau tanda tangan developer cocok; kalau tidak, halaman rilis yang dibuka.

        How do I uninstall it?Bagaimana cara menghapus Amnesia?

        Settings → Uninstall Amnesia, or Apps & features like any app. It turns off first, so nothing more is wiped. Your Keep folder always stays. If Keep is locked it asks for the Keep password, and it warns you if the safe box still has files. To delete settings, Keep List and vault too, type DELETE in the box.Pengaturan → Uninstall Amnesia, atau lewat Apps & features seperti app lain. Amnesia mati dulu, jadi tidak ada lagi yang dihapus. Folder Keep selalu tetap ada. Kalau Keep terkunci, ia meminta password Keep, dan memperingatkan kalau brankas masih berisi file. Untuk ikut menghapus pengaturan, Keep List, dan vault, ketik HAPUS di kotaknya.

        Do Microsoft Store apps and new programs get removed?Apakah app Microsoft Store dan program baru ikut dihapus?

        Only with "Remove new apps" on. Then a program installed just for you after Turn On is removed at sign out, and its folder waits 3 days in Amnesia's trash first. Press Keep to keep it. Store apps keep their own data unless you switch on "Also wipe Store app data".Hanya kalau "Hapus app baru" menyala. Program yang dipasang khusus untuk kamu setelah Amnesia dinyalakan dihapus saat sign out, dan foldernya menunggu 3 hari dulu di tempat sampah Amnesia. Tekan Simpan untuk menyimpannya. App Store tetap menyimpan datanya, kecuali kamu nyalakan "Hapus juga data app Store".

        Is there a Mac version?Ada versi Mac?

        Yes, and it's been around longer. See the Mac page.Ada, dan sudah lebih lama. Lihat halaman Mac.

        Found a bug? Want something changed?Ketemu bug? Mau ada yang diubah?

        Open an issue on GitHub. Please don't paste passwords, private file lists or anything personal. Collect log leaves file names out for that reason.Buka issue di GitHub. Tolong jangan tempel password, daftar file pribadi, atau hal pribadi lainnya. Kumpulkan log sengaja tidak menyertakan nama file.
