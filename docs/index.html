02 · Your Mac, every day

All of this, every day?

Every day your Mac quietly keeps a record of what you did. The browser saves your history and cookies. Downloads, Desktop and Documents fill up with files you only needed once. Apps write caches and logs, and Terminal remembers every command you typed.

It all piles up, day after day, until you clean it by hand. Or never.

03 · Log out

Log out. Forgotten.

Press Log Out, restart or shut down and Amnesia wipes all of it. Only what you chose stays: your Keep folder, the apps on your Keep List and the logins in your Profile Vault. Your installed apps stay too.

At the next login it checks once more, so even a crash or a power cut doesn't leave traces behind.

Selective Amnesia is the name for this: everything is forgotten, except what you chose to keep.

    04 · What stays

    What gets wiped, what stays.

    Nothing is deleted until you press Turn On, and you can always check the list first. Search it, or put the biggest files on top.

    Wiped at logout

    • Browser history and cookies
    • Desktop, Downloads, Documents, Pictures, Movies, Music
    • Caches, logs, Terminal history
    • Everything in the Trash
    • Data of apps that are not in the vault or on the Keep List

    Stays safe

    • Everything in your Keep folder (any place, any name)
    • Logins saved in the Profile Vault (back with Restore)
    • Apps on your Keep List (VPN, SSH keys, Apple account…)
    • Virtual machines (UTM, Parallels, VirtualBox, VMware Fusion), after you press Keep
    • Your installed apps themselves
    password ••••••••••••

    05 · Profile Vault

    One password. Every login back.

    An encrypted safe for your app logins. It's what lets you log in to a clean Mac and have Chrome, WhatsApp, Claude and your coding tools ready in one step.

    • AES-256. Locked with 7-Zip. Forget the password and nobody can open it.
    • Light snapshots. Browsers saved without caches: about 1 GB becomes 150 MB.
    • Save & Log Out. One click saves your logins, then logs out. If saving fails, the logout is cancelled.
    • Undo Restore. Data on your Mac newer than the snapshot? You're asked first. Anything on your Keep List is never overwritten, and until your next logout one button puts back what was there before.

    Memory Recall Restore Profiles brings every login back after a clean logout.

    Global Amnesia 3 wrong passwords or your panic word, and the vault is gone for good.

    07 · Backup

    Backup anywhere.

    An encrypted .7z copy (AES-256, file names too) of your Keep folder, chosen folders and the Profile Vault.

    • USB drive, your own server over SSH, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box or pCloud. Daily or weekly, and you can check a backup file.
    • Every backup is a new dated file. Old backups are never deleted by Amnesia.

    Memory Recall Restore Files puts files back where they came from. Get Vault from Backup brings a lost vault back.

    Log out, log back in, hand it over. Browser history, logins, recent files and chats are already gone. Your vault stays locked.

    Nosy family?

    Your browser history doesn't survive a logout. Nobody needs to know about that 3 a.m. shopping spree.

    "Just open it real quick."

    Logging in shows an empty desktop. Asked for the vault password too? Type your panic word instead. The vault is gone, and the Keep folder too if you chose so. No drama.

    Lost or stolen?

    After a logout your history, logins and chats are already gone, and the vault is AES-256. Turn on FileVault too, so your Keep folder is encrypted on the disk.

    $ amnesia --forget-everything # pun intended

    Honest fine print: Amnesia protects you after a logout. While you're logged in, your Mac is as open as always, so lock your screen. And the panic word can't be undone.

    Brankas (experimental)

    An encrypted disk (AES-256) inside your Keep folder for files that must stay secret. Amnesia closes it before every cleanup and tells you if it couldn't. Forget its password and the files are gone.

    Signed updates

    Updates from inside the app only install when the release's SHA256SUMS.txt has a valid signature. If it doesn't, you're sent to the release page instead.

    Safe with iCloud

    With iCloud Desktop & Documents on, those two folders are skipped, so nothing gets deleted from iCloud or your other devices. Folders that are symlinks are never emptied through the link.

    Claude and Claude Code

    Put Claude on your Keep List and your Claude Code chats (~/.claude) stay too. Before logout, apps ticked in the vault get a fresh snapshot, so a restore is never out of date.

    Care. Space back, with a list first.

    Amnesia already wipes most of your home folder at every logout, so Care is for what stays: your Keep folder, the apps you kept and big files from this session. Every action scans first and shows a list, and nothing risky is ticked for you.

    Disk overview

    See what takes space: Keep folder, vault, trash, caches, the biggest folders and files, old downloads. Read only, nothing is deleted here.

    Clean now

    Empties caches, logs and Amnesia's trash. Caches of apps that are open are skipped. Emptying the Trash is listed, but never ticked for you.

    Uninstall apps

    Moves an app you installed and its caches to the Trash. Its personal data stays unless you tick it.

    Project build folders

    node_modules, target, build, dist and more can go to the Trash. Folders used in the last 7 days or tracked by git are left alone.

    Old installers

    Old .dmg and .pkg files go to the Trash. Disk images and virtual machines are never picked for you.

    Safe tune-ups

    Refresh Quick Look thumbnails or restart the Dock, without an admin password.

    Stops when unsure

    If the Keep List can't be read or has gone missing, the cleanup deletes nothing and says so in History. When a check can't tell, the item is kept.

    Every run in History

    Every Care run is in History with the space freed. The Health check also shows free disk space, memory and battery condition.

    Modes and profiles. You choose how much is forgotten.

    Pick one mode for every logout, restore only the apps you need, and ask an AI for advice without sharing your files. Every change shows a preview first and takes effect at the next logout.

    Retrograde Amnesia

    Like a brand-new account at every login. Only your Keep folder and what Amnesia needs to work stay. Auto snapshot at logout is turned off, and you can still restore a snapshot by hand.

    Selective Amnesia (recommended)

    The basic list for a new install: your Keep folder, app logins from the vault, SSH keys, Git and shell settings. Add your own apps on the Keep List.

    Partial Amnesia

    Apps keep their settings and logins in place. Your files, Downloads, Desktop, caches, logs, the Trash and shell history are wiped. Browsing history and cookies inside a browser's own folder stay too, because that folder is kept.

    Custom Amnesia

    Your own Keep List. Picked for you when you change the lines a mode added. A mode only changes its own marked section, so your own lines always stay.

    A preview before every switch

    See what will now be wiped and what will now be kept, with a warning when more is wiped. Nothing is deleted when you switch. A missing or damaged mode file never empties your Keep List.

    Vault profiles

    Restore only what you need, like Work or Personal, or everything. Rename, add and remove profiles. One browser holds one set of logins, so a second browser keeps personal use apart.

    Ask an AI

    Amnesia makes a report with no file contents and no names of your files, plus a ready prompt. Paste it into the AI you choose, paste the answer back and tick the changes you want. Amnesia itself sends nothing.

    Terminal command

    Switch it on in Settings for a read-only amnesia command: status, preview, report, keep and history. It never deletes or changes anything.

        12 · FAQ

        Questions, answered.

        Safety

        Is my data sent anywhere?

        No. Amnesia has no servers, no accounts, no tracking and no analytics. Everything runs on your Mac. Only if you set up a server or cloud backup does a locked .7z file leave your Mac, straight to the place you picked, and nobody can read it without your password. The update check only asks GitHub for the newest version number, once a week by default. In Settings you can make it every 2 weeks, every month, or Off.

        Is there a Windows version?

        Yes, Amnesia for Windows v0.11.0, an official release. It wipes at sign out, restart and shutdown, and has the vault, backups, Keep lock, a safe box, a Health check and a Setup.exe with signed updates from inside the app. See the Windows page.

        Can I try it without deleting anything?

        Yes. Amnesia stays off until you press Turn On. What Gets Deleted (or Settings → "See what would be deleted now") shows the full list first.

        Where do wiped files go? Can I get them back?

        At logout they're moved into Amnesia's own folder (~/.amnesia-trash), which is emptied in the background after your next login. They don't go to the macOS Trash and can't be brought back. Anything you need goes into the Keep folder, the Keep List or the vault.

        Is my Keep folder encrypted?

        Not by Amnesia. Keep lock stops Finder and other apps from opening it until you type the Keep password, but it's macOS permissions, not encryption. Turn on FileVault so the whole disk is encrypted, and put files that must stay secret in the Brankas.

        What if the Mac crashes or loses power?

        The check at login catches it: if the last logout didn't finish, the login check does the full cleanup.

        Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

        So macOS doesn't ask about every folder (Desktop, Documents, Downloads…), and so the wipe at logout can actually reach them. You allow it once.

        I use iCloud Desktop & Documents. Is that a problem?

        Amnesia skips Desktop and Documents then, because wiping them would delete the files from iCloud and your other devices too. The Health check and What Gets Deleted tell you. If you want them wiped, turn that iCloud option off first.

        Does it need my Mac password or sudo?

        No. Amnesia only works inside your own user account and never uses sudo. macOS asks for your login password only when you save Keychain keys for a move to a new Mac.

        Backup

        Do I need Terminal or Chrome to set up backups?

        No. For a server, type its password once and Amnesia installs an SSH key. For Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box or pCloud, you log in through your browser.

        How do I know a backup really works?

        On the Backup page press Check a Backup File and pick the .7z. Amnesia opens the vault inside it in a temporary folder, shows which apps are in it, then deletes the temporary folder. Your own vault isn't touched.

        Does the scheduled backup work when I'm not looking?

        Yes, while the Amnesia icon is in the menu bar, with the password saved in Keychain. For a USB drive it waits until the drive is plugged in. You get a notification each time. "Run the scheduled backup now" tests it right away.

        How do I get files back from a backup?

        Backup → Restore Files: pick the .7z, tick the folders or files, then press Restore to Original Place. Amnesia remembers where each folder came from (also on an external drive). Anything already there is moved to ~/.amnesia/before-restore first, nothing is overwritten. Drive not plugged in? Use To Another Folder.

        Why can't I open a backup by double-clicking it?

        The backup is locked with AES-256, and even its list of file names is encrypted. The Mac's built-in Archive Utility can't open that. Use Restore Files in Amnesia, the free app Keka, or Terminal: ~/.amnesia/bin/7zz x file.7z (type the password when it asks, don't put it in the command).

        Check a Backup File says the backup has no vault. Is it broken?

        No. It was made with Include Profile Vault off (or before v5.13). Your files in it are fine. If the password is wrong, the message says so separately.

        The scheduled backup asked for my Mac password. Why?

        macOS asks once when an older Amnesia saved the backup password in Keychain. Press Always Allow. From v5.17 Amnesia saves it again as its own, so it won't ask again.

        Are old backups deleted?

        Never. Every backup is a new dated file. Delete old ones yourself when you don't need them.

        Profile Vault

        What if I forget the vault password?

        Then nobody can open it, including you. Make a new vault and log in to your apps again. Still know it and want a new one? Use the Password button on the Profile Vault page.

        What happens after 3 wrong passwords?

        The vault is deleted for good. Before the last try Amnesia warns you. If the vault was in a backup, get it back on Move to a New Mac → Get Vault from Backup.

        What's the difference between light and full?

        Light (browsers only) leaves out caches and the program files of Web Store extensions, so Chrome goes from about 1 GB to 150 MB. Logins, bookmarks, extension settings and extensions you installed another way are always saved. After a light Restore, press Repair on the browser's extensions page. Full saves everything except caches.

        Does Save & Log Out overwrite my old snapshots?

        Only for the apps it saves, and only after the new snapshot is saved successfully. Apps not in the list keep their snapshot. If saving fails, you're not logged out.

        What does the auto snapshot do?

        When you log out, restart or shut down the normal way, Amnesia first saves the apps whose data changed (at most 150 seconds), then cleans. It's skipped if you just used Save & Log Out or Pause 1 Session is on. Turn it off and a normal logout cleans without saving.

        I restored and my newer data went back. Can I undo it?

        Yes, until your next logout. Press Undo Restore on the Profile Vault page and the data from just before the restore comes back. Since v5.23 Amnesia also asks first when an app's data is newer than its snapshot, and never overwrites what's on your Keep List.

        What is the panic word?

        An optional word you choose. Typed in the password box, it deletes the vault right away (and, if you chose so, empties the Keep folder too).

        Everyday use

        I need my files tomorrow. What do I do?

        Move them into your Keep folder before you log out. Or, for a single day, press Pause 1 Session: the next logout and login are not wiped.

        Can I cancel a logout?

        Yes, with "Check before logout" on (Settings): logout, restart and shutdown wait on the What Gets Deleted page until you press Continue or Cancel.

        The main window opens at login. Why?

        Older versions were reopened by macOS's "Reopen windows when logging back in". Since v5.15 Amnesia only shows its menu bar icon at login.

        I use virtual machines. Are they wiped?

        Not by default. When Amnesia finds UTM, Parallels, VirtualBox or VMware Fusion data, it puts it on the Keep List once. Switch it off and it stays off, and the row says it's wiped at logout. What Gets Deleted also warns you about files of 1 GB or more.

        Is the Trash emptied too?

        Yes, at every logout. What Gets Deleted shows it as its own group, "Trash (emptied at logout)", with its size. Take back what you still need first.

        How do I update?

        Press Turn Off, install the new version (.dmg or brew upgrade), open Amnesia, press Turn On.

        Is an update from inside the app safe?

        It only installs when the release's SHA256SUMS.txt has a valid signature from the developer key built into Amnesia. If the signature is missing or wrong, it opens the release page instead and installs nothing.

        Can I move to a new Mac?

        Yes, the Move to a New Mac page walks you through it. Prepare Move saves your Keychain keys to the vault, a backup carries it over, and Restore Keys puts them back on the new Mac.

        Uninstall

        How do I uninstall it?

        Open Settings and press Uninstall Amnesia. It turns Amnesia off for good and moves the app to the Trash. A checkbox lets you delete the vault, snapshots, Keep List and settings too (moved to the Trash as well). Your Keep folder is never touched. Prefer Terminal? bash ~/.amnesia/uninstall.sh (add --all to delete the vault too). With Homebrew: brew uninstall amnesia.

        Keep lock is on. What happens when I uninstall?

        Amnesia asks for the Keep password and unlocks Keep first. Without the password Keep stays locked, and you get the command that opens it: chmod -RN ~/Keep.

        What if I just drag it to the Trash?

        That's safe too. If Amnesia is open, it notices, turns itself off and tells you. If it isn't, the login agent checks at the next logout, wipes nothing and removes itself.
