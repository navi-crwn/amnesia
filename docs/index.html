Your Mac forgets everything.Except what you keep. Mac kamu lupa semuanya.Kecuali yang kamu simpan.

Every time you log out, Amnesia wipes your history, downloads and app data. Your logins, and the files you choose, stay safe in an encrypted vault. Setiap kali kamu logout, Amnesia menghapus riwayat, download dan data app. Login kamu, dan file yang kamu pilih, tetap aman di brankas terenkripsi.

01 · Your Mac, every dayMac kamu, setiap hari

All of this, every day?Semua ini, setiap hari?

Every day your Mac quietly keeps a record of what you did. The browser saves your history and cookies. Downloads, Desktop and Documents fill up with files you only needed once. Apps write caches and logs, and Terminal remembers every command you typed.Setiap hari Mac diam-diam mencatat apa yang kamu lakukan. Browser menyimpan riwayat dan cookie. Downloads, Desktop, dan Documents penuh file yang cuma kamu perlukan sekali. App menulis cache dan log, dan Terminal ingat setiap perintah yang kamu ketik.

It all piles up, day after day, until you clean it by hand. Or never.Semuanya menumpuk hari demi hari, sampai kamu bersihkan sendiri. Atau tidak pernah sama sekali.

02 · Log outLogout

Log out. Forgotten.Logout. Terlupakan.

Press Log Out, restart or shut down and Amnesia wipes all of it. Only what you chose stays: your Keep folder, the apps on your Keep List and the logins in your Profile Vault. Your installed apps stay too.Tekan Logout, restart, atau shutdown, dan Amnesia menghapus semuanya. Yang tersisa hanya yang kamu pilih: folder Keep, app di Keep List, dan login di Profile Vault. App yang terpasang juga tetap ada.

At the next login it checks once more, so even a crash or a power cut doesn't leave traces behind.Saat login berikutnya Amnesia mengecek sekali lagi, jadi crash atau mati listrik pun tidak meninggalkan jejak.

Selective Amnesia is the name for this: everything is forgotten, except what you chose to keep.adalah nama untuk ini: semuanya dilupakan, kecuali yang kamu pilih untuk disimpan.

    04 · Profile Vault

    One password. Every login back.1 password. Semua login kembali.

    An encrypted safe for your app logins. It's what lets you log in to a clean Mac and have Chrome, WhatsApp, Claude and your coding tools ready in one step.Brankas terenkripsi untuk login app kamu. Inilah yang membuat Mac yang sudah bersih bisa langsung dipakai: Chrome, WhatsApp, Claude, dan alat coding siap dalam 1 langkah.

    • AES-256. Locked with 7-Zip. Forget the password and nobody can open it.Dikunci dengan 7-Zip. Lupa password, tidak ada yang bisa membukanya.
    • Light snapshots.Snapshot ringan. Browsers saved without caches: about 1 GB becomes 150 MB.Browser disimpan tanpa cache: sekitar 1 GB jadi 150 MB.
    • Save & Log Out.Simpan & Logout. One click saves your logins, then logs out. If saving fails, the logout is cancelled.1 klik menyimpan login, lalu logout. Kalau gagal menyimpan, logout dibatalkan.

    Memory Recall Restore Profiles brings every login back after a clean logout.Restore Profil mengembalikan semua login setelah logout bersih.

    Global Amnesia 3 wrong passwords or your panic word, and the vault is gone for good.Salah password 3 kali atau kata panik, dan vault hilang selamanya.

    06 · Backup

    Backup anywhere.Backup ke mana saja.

    An encrypted .7z copy (AES-256, file names too) of your Keep folder, chosen folders and the Profile Vault.Salinan .7z terenkripsi (AES-256, nama file juga) dari folder Keep, folder pilihan, dan Profile Vault.

    Memory Recall Restore Files puts files back where they came from. Get Vault from Backup brings a lost vault back.Pulihkan File mengembalikan file ke tempat asalnya. Ambil Vault dari Backup mengembalikan vault yang hilang.

      01 / 10

      Open Amnesia and read the warningBuka Amnesia dan baca peringatannya

      The first time Amnesia opens, a short tour starts. Pick your language, read what Amnesia deletes, and tick that you understand. Nothing is deleted yet.Saat Amnesia pertama dibuka, tur singkat dimulai. Pilih bahasa, baca apa yang dihapus Amnesia, lalu centang bahwa kamu paham. Belum ada yang dihapus.

      • You can open the tour again later from Settings.Tur ini bisa dibuka lagi nanti dari Pengaturan.

      02 / 10

      Quick checkCek dulu

      Amnesia checks what it needs: its engine, 7-Zip, Apple Command Line Tools and Full Disk Access. Anything that isn't green has a button next to it.Amnesia mengecek yang dibutuhkan: mesinnya, 7-Zip, Apple Command Line Tools, dan Akses Disk Penuh. Yang belum hijau ada tombol di sebelahnya.

      • Full Disk Access: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access → switch Amnesia on, then Quit & Reopen.Akses Disk Penuh: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access → nyalakan Amnesia, lalu Tutup & Buka Lagi.
      • It lets the cleanup reach Desktop, Documents and Downloads.Izin ini supaya pembersihan bisa menjangkau Desktop, Documents, dan Downloads.

      03 / 10

      Pick what staysPilih yang tetap ada

      Choose your Keep folder (~/Keep by default) and switch on the apps whose data should never be wiped. VPN apps are suggested, and your Apple account is ticked for you.Pilih folder Keep (bawaannya ~/Keep) dan nyalakan app yang datanya tidak boleh dihapus. App VPN sudah disarankan, dan akun Apple sudah dicentang.

      • You can change this later on the Keep List page.Nanti bisa diubah di halaman Keep List.

      04 / 10

      Create the Profile VaultBuat Profile Vault

      Open Profile Vault and type a password of at least 12 characters, twice. A panic word is optional.Buka Profile Vault dan ketik password minimal 12 karakter, dua kali. Kata panik boleh diisi, boleh tidak.

      • Write the password down somewhere safe. Nobody can recover it.Catat password di tempat aman. Tidak ada yang bisa memulihkannya.
      • Global Amnesia typing the panic word deletes the vault for good.mengetik kata panik menghapus vault selamanya.

      05 / 10

      Pick apps, make the first snapshotPilih app, buat snapshot pertama

      Click the apps to save, choose Light or Full for each browser, log in to those apps as usual, then press Snapshot Now.Klik app yang mau disimpan, pilih Ringan atau Lengkap untuk tiap browser, login ke app itu seperti biasa, lalu tekan Snapshot Sekarang.

      • Snapshot Now needs no password.Snapshot Sekarang tidak butuh password.
      • Light skips caches: about 1 GB becomes 150 MB.Ringan tanpa cache: sekitar 1 GB jadi 150 MB.

      06 / 10

      Back up, with the vaultBackup, termasuk vault

      Pick where backups go, keep Include Profile Vault ticked and press Back Up Now. Then Check a Backup File to be sure the vault inside opens.Pilih tujuan backup, biarkan Sertakan Profile Vault tercentang, lalu tekan Backup Sekarang. Lalu Cek File Backup untuk memastikan vault di dalamnya bisa dibuka.

      • Memory Recall if the vault is ever lost, Get Vault from Backup brings it back.kalau vault suatu saat hilang, Ambil Vault dari Backup mengembalikannya.
      • Every backup is a new dated file. Old ones are never deleted.Setiap backup jadi file baru bertanggal. Yang lama tidak pernah dihapus.

      07 / 10

      Look at the listLihat daftarnya

      What Gets Deleted shows everything the next logout would delete, grouped by meaning. Still need something? Move it into your Keep folder or add the app to the Keep List.Yang Akan Dihapus menampilkan semua yang akan dihapus saat logout berikutnya, dikelompokkan menurut artinya. Masih ada yang perlu? Pindahkan ke folder Keep atau tambahkan app-nya ke Keep List.

      • Opening the list deletes nothing.Membuka daftar ini tidak menghapus apa pun.

      08 / 10

      Turn OnAktifkan

      Press Turn On Amnesia on the home card and read the popup. This session stays safe until you log out.Tekan Aktifkan Amnesia di kartu halaman utama dan baca popup-nya. Sesi sekarang tetap aman sampai kamu logout.

      • Selective Amnesia from now on, every logout, restart and shutdown forgets everything except what you chose.mulai sekarang, setiap logout, restart, dan shutdown melupakan semuanya kecuali yang kamu pilih.

      09 / 10

      Every day: Save & Log Out, then RestoreSetiap hari: Simpan & Logout, lalu Restore

      Done for the day? Press Save & Log Out in the menu bar. After you log in again, open Profile Vault, type the password and press Restore Profiles.Selesai kerja? Tekan Simpan & Logout di menu bar. Setelah login lagi, buka Profile Vault, ketik password, lalu tekan Restore Profil.

      • Memory Recall Chrome, Claude, WhatsApp and the rest come back logged in.Chrome, Claude, WhatsApp, dan lainnya kembali dalam keadaan login.
      • Saved light? Press Repair on the browser's extensions page.Disimpan ringan? Tekan Repair di halaman extension browser.

      10 / 10

      Updating AmnesiaUpdate Amnesia

      Always in this order: Turn Off, install the update (.dmg or brew upgrade), open Amnesia, Turn On.Selalu dengan urutan ini: Matikan, pasang update (.dmg atau brew upgrade), buka Amnesia, Aktifkan.

      • Installing while Amnesia is on can be treated like a logout and wipe the session.Memasang saat Amnesia aktif bisa dianggap seperti logout dan membersihkan sesi.

        Nothing is deleted before step 8.Tidak ada yang dihapus sebelum langkah 8.

        11 · FAQ

        Questions, answered.Pertanyaan, terjawab.

        SafetyKeamanan

        Is my data sent anywhere?Apakah data saya dikirim ke mana-mana?

        No. Amnesia has no servers, no accounts, no tracking and no analytics. Everything runs on your Mac. Only if you set up a server or cloud backup does a locked .7z file leave your Mac, straight to the place you picked, and nobody can read it without your password.Tidak. Amnesia tidak punya server, tidak pakai akun, tidak melacak, dan tidak mengumpulkan data. Semua jalan di Mac kamu. Hanya kalau kamu mengatur backup ke server atau cloud, file .7z terkunci keluar dari Mac, langsung ke tempat yang kamu pilih, dan tidak ada yang bisa membacanya tanpa password kamu.

        Can I try it without deleting anything?Bisa dicoba tanpa menghapus apa pun?

        Yes. Amnesia stays off until you press Turn On. What Gets Deleted (or Settings → "See what would be deleted now") shows the full list first.Bisa. Amnesia tetap mati sampai kamu menekan Aktifkan. Yang Akan Dihapus (atau Pengaturan → "Lihat yang akan dihapus sekarang") menampilkan daftarnya dulu.

        Where do wiped files go? Can I get them back?File yang dibersihkan ke mana? Bisa dikembalikan?

        At logout they're moved into Amnesia's own folder (~/.amnesia-trash), which is emptied in the background after your next login. They don't go to the macOS Trash and can't be brought back. Anything you need goes into the Keep folder, the Keep List or the vault.Saat logout semuanya dipindah ke folder milik Amnesia (~/.amnesia-trash), yang dikosongkan di background setelah login berikutnya. Tidak masuk Trash macOS dan tidak bisa dikembalikan. Yang masih perlu, simpan di folder Keep, Keep List, atau vault.

        What if the Mac crashes or loses power?Kalau Mac crash atau mati listrik?

        The check at login catches it: if the last logout didn't finish, the login check does the full cleanup.Cek saat login menanganinya: kalau logout terakhir tidak selesai, cek saat login membersihkan penuh.

        Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?Kenapa minta Akses Disk Penuh?

        So macOS doesn't ask about every folder (Desktop, Documents, Downloads…), and so the wipe at logout can actually reach them. You allow it once.Supaya macOS tidak bertanya per folder (Desktop, Documents, Downloads…), dan supaya pembersihan saat logout benar-benar bisa menjangkaunya. Cukup izinkan sekali.

        Does it need my Mac password or sudo?Apakah butuh password Mac atau sudo?

        No. Amnesia only works inside your own user account and never uses sudo. macOS asks for your login password only when you save Keychain keys for a move to a new Mac.Tidak. Amnesia hanya bekerja di dalam akun kamu sendiri dan tidak pernah memakai sudo. macOS meminta password login hanya saat kamu menyimpan kunci Keychain untuk pindah ke Mac baru.

        Backup

        Do I need Terminal or Chrome to set up backups?Perlu Terminal atau Chrome untuk mengatur backup?

        No. For a server, type its password once and Amnesia installs an SSH key. For Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box or pCloud, you log in through your browser.Tidak. Untuk server, ketik password-nya sekali dan Amnesia memasang kunci SSH. Untuk Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, atau pCloud, kamu login lewat browser.

        How do I know a backup really works?Bagaimana tahu backup benar-benar bisa dipakai?

        On the Backup page press Check a Backup File and pick the .7z. Amnesia opens the vault inside it in a temporary folder, shows which apps are in it, then deletes the temporary folder. Your own vault isn't touched.Di halaman Backup tekan Cek File Backup lalu pilih file .7z-nya. Amnesia membuka vault di dalamnya di folder sementara, menampilkan app apa saja yang ada, lalu menghapus folder sementara itu. Vault kamu sendiri tidak disentuh.

        Does the scheduled backup work when I'm not looking?Apakah backup terjadwal jalan sendiri?

        Yes, while the Amnesia icon is in the menu bar, with the password saved in Keychain. For a USB drive it waits until the drive is plugged in. You get a notification each time. "Run the scheduled backup now" tests it right away.Ya, selama ikon Amnesia ada di menu bar, dengan password tersimpan di Keychain. Untuk flashdisk, backup menunggu sampai flashdisk tercolok. Setiap kali selesai muncul notifikasi. "Jalankan backup terjadwal sekarang" untuk langsung mengetesnya.

        How do I get files back from a backup?Bagaimana mengembalikan file dari backup?

        Backup → Restore Files: pick the .7z, tick the folders or files, then press Restore to Original Place. Amnesia remembers where each folder came from (also on an external drive). Anything already there is moved to ~/.amnesia/before-restore first, nothing is overwritten. Drive not plugged in? Use To Another Folder.Backup → Pulihkan File: pilih file .7z, centang folder atau file-nya, lalu tekan Pulihkan ke Lokasi Asal. Amnesia ingat asal tiap folder (juga yang di drive eksternal). Yang sudah ada di tempat itu dipindah dulu ke ~/.amnesia/before-restore, tidak ada yang ditimpa. Drive belum dicolok? Pakai Ke Folder Lain.

        Why can't I open a backup by double-clicking it?Kenapa backup tidak bisa dibuka dengan klik dua kali?

        The backup is locked with AES-256, and even its list of file names is encrypted. The Mac's built-in Archive Utility can't open that. Use Restore Files in Amnesia, the free app Keka, or Terminal: ~/.amnesia/bin/7zz x file.7z (type the password when it asks, don't put it in the command).Backup dikunci AES-256, bahkan daftar nama file-nya ikut dikunci. Archive Utility bawaan Mac tidak bisa membukanya. Pakai Pulihkan File di Amnesia, app gratis Keka, atau Terminal: ~/.amnesia/bin/7zz x file.7z (ketik password saat diminta, jangan ditulis di perintah).

        Check a Backup File says the backup has no vault. Is it broken?Cek File Backup bilang backup tidak berisi vault. Apakah rusak?

        No. It was made with Include Profile Vault off (or before v5.13). Your files in it are fine. If the password is wrong, the message says so separately.Tidak. Backup itu dibuat saat Sertakan Profile Vault mati (atau sebelum v5.13). File lain di dalamnya tetap aman. Kalau password-nya yang salah, pesannya akan menyebut itu secara terpisah.

        The scheduled backup asked for my Mac password. Why?Backup terjadwal meminta password Mac. Kenapa?

        macOS asks once when an older Amnesia saved the backup password in Keychain. Press Always Allow. From v5.17 Amnesia saves it again as its own, so it won't ask again.macOS bertanya sekali kalau password backup disimpan oleh versi Amnesia yang lama. Tekan Always Allow. Mulai v5.17 Amnesia menyimpannya ulang sebagai miliknya, jadi tidak bertanya lagi.

        Are old backups deleted?Apakah backup lama dihapus?

        Never. Every backup is a new dated file. Delete old ones yourself when you don't need them.Tidak pernah. Setiap backup jadi file baru bertanggal. Hapus sendiri yang lama kalau sudah tidak perlu.

        Profile Vault

        What if I forget the vault password?Kalau lupa password vault?

        Then nobody can open it, including you. Make a new vault and log in to your apps again. Still know it and want a new one? Use the Password button on the Profile Vault page.Tidak ada yang bisa membukanya, termasuk kamu. Buat vault baru, lalu login ulang ke app kamu. Masih ingat dan mau ganti? Pakai tombol Password di halaman Profile Vault.

        What happens after 3 wrong passwords?Apa yang terjadi kalau salah password 3 kali?

        The vault is deleted for good. Before the last try Amnesia warns you. If the vault was in a backup, get it back on Move to a New Mac → Get Vault from Backup.Vault dihapus selamanya. Sebelum percobaan terakhir, Amnesia memberi peringatan. Kalau vault pernah ikut backup, ambil lagi di Pindah Mac → Ambil Vault dari Backup.

        What's the difference between light and full?Apa bedanya ringan dan lengkap?

        Light (browsers only) leaves out caches and the program files of Web Store extensions, so Chrome goes from about 1 GB to 150 MB. Logins, bookmarks, extension settings and extensions you installed another way are always saved. After a light Restore, press Repair on the browser's extensions page. Full saves everything except caches.Ringan (hanya browser) tidak menyimpan cache dan file program extension dari Web Store, jadi Chrome dari sekitar 1 GB jadi 150 MB. Login, bookmark, pengaturan extension, dan extension yang dipasang dengan cara lain selalu disimpan. Setelah Restore ringan, tekan Repair di halaman extension browser. Lengkap menyimpan semuanya kecuali cache.

        Does Save & Log Out overwrite my old snapshots?Apakah Simpan & Logout menimpa snapshot lama?

        Only for the apps it saves, and only after the new snapshot is saved successfully. Apps not in the list keep their snapshot. If saving fails, you're not logged out.Hanya untuk app yang disimpan, dan hanya setelah snapshot baru berhasil disimpan. App yang tidak ada di daftar tetap memakai snapshot lamanya. Kalau gagal menyimpan, kamu tidak di-logout.

        What does the auto snapshot do?Apa fungsi snapshot otomatis?

        When you log out, restart or shut down the normal way, Amnesia first saves the apps whose data changed (at most 150 seconds), then cleans. It's skipped if you just used Save & Log Out or Pause 1 Session is on. Turn it off and a normal logout cleans without saving.Saat kamu logout, restart, atau mematikan Mac dengan cara biasa, Amnesia lebih dulu menyimpan app yang datanya berubah (paling lama 150 detik), baru membersihkan. Dilewati kalau kamu baru saja menekan Simpan & Logout atau sedang Jeda 1 Sesi. Kalau dimatikan, logout biasa langsung membersihkan tanpa menyimpan.

        What is the panic word?Apa itu kata panik?

        An optional word you choose. Typed in the password box, it deletes the vault right away (and, if you chose so, empties the Keep folder too).Kata opsional pilihan kamu. Kalau diketik di kolom password, vault langsung dihapus (dan, kalau kamu pilih, folder Keep juga dikosongkan).

        Everyday usePemakaian sehari-hari

        I need my files tomorrow. What do I do?File ini masih saya perlukan besok. Bagaimana?

        Move them into your Keep folder before you log out. Or, for a single day, press Pause 1 Session: the next logout and login are not wiped.Pindahkan ke folder Keep sebelum logout. Atau, untuk sehari saja, tekan Jeda 1 Sesi: logout dan login berikutnya tidak dibersihkan.

        Can I cancel a logout?Bisa membatalkan logout?

        Yes, with "Check before logout" on (Settings): logout, restart and shutdown wait on the What Gets Deleted page until you press Continue or Cancel.Bisa, kalau "Cek dulu sebelum logout" nyala (Pengaturan): logout, restart, dan shutdown menunggu di halaman Yang Akan Dihapus sampai kamu menekan Lanjut atau Batal.

        The main window opens at login. Why?Jendela utama terbuka saat login. Kenapa?

        Older versions were reopened by macOS's "Reopen windows when logging back in". Since v5.15 Amnesia only shows its menu bar icon at login.Versi lama dibuka ulang oleh fitur macOS "Reopen windows when logging back in". Sejak v5.15 Amnesia hanya menampilkan ikon menu bar saat login.

        How do I update?Bagaimana cara update?

        Press Turn Off, install the new version (.dmg or brew upgrade), open Amnesia, press Turn On.Tekan Matikan, pasang versi baru (.dmg atau brew upgrade), buka Amnesia, tekan Aktifkan.

        Can I move to a new Mac?Bisa pindah ke Mac baru?

        Yes, the Move to a New Mac page walks you through it. Prepare Move saves your Keychain keys to the vault, a backup carries it over, and Restore Keys puts them back on the new Mac.Bisa, halaman Pindah Mac memandu langkahnya. Siapkan Pindah menyimpan kunci Keychain ke vault, backup membawanya, lalu Pulihkan Kunci memasangnya di Mac baru.

        UninstallMenghapus Amnesia

        How do I uninstall it?Cara menghapus Amnesia?

        Open Settings and press Uninstall Amnesia. It turns Amnesia off for good and moves the app to the Trash. A checkbox lets you delete the vault, snapshots, Keep List and settings too (moved to the Trash as well). Your Keep folder is never touched. Prefer Terminal? bash ~/.amnesia/uninstall.sh (add --all to delete the vault too). With Homebrew: brew uninstall --cask amnesia.Buka Pengaturan lalu tekan Hapus Amnesia. Amnesia dimatikan untuk seterusnya dan app dipindah ke Trash. Ada kotak centang untuk ikut menghapus vault, snapshot, Keep List, dan pengaturan (juga dipindah ke Trash). Folder Keep tidak pernah disentuh. Lebih suka Terminal? bash ~/.amnesia/uninstall.sh (tambah --all untuk ikut menghapus vault). Lewat Homebrew: brew uninstall --cask amnesia.

        What if I just drag it to the Trash?Kalau app cuma diseret ke Trash?

        That's safe too. If Amnesia is open, it notices, turns itself off and tells you. If it isn't, the login agent checks at the next logout, wipes nothing and removes itself.Itu juga aman. Kalau Amnesia sedang terbuka, ia tahu, mematikan dirinya sendiri, lalu memberi tahu kamu. Kalau tidak terbuka, agent login mengecek saat logout berikutnya, tidak membersihkan apa pun, lalu menghapus dirinya sendiri.
