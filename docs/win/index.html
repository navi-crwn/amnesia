02 · Your PC, every day

All of this, every day?

Your browser saves history and cookies. Downloads and Desktop fill up with files you needed once. And Windows keeps its own notes: recent files, what you typed in Run and Explorer, search history, thumbnails, Notepad tabs, clipboard history.

It piles up until you clean it by hand. Or never.

03 · Sign out

Sign out. Forgotten.

Sign out, restart or shut down and Amnesia wipes all of it. Amnesia starts cleaning the moment Windows asks "may I sign out?", and Windows shows "Amnesia is cleaning up..." until it's done. Only what you chose stays: your Keep folder, your Keep List and the logins in your vault. Your installed programs stay too.

At the next sign in it checks once more, so a crash or a power cut doesn't leave traces behind.

Selective Amnesia is the name for this: everything is forgotten, except what you chose to keep.

    04 · What stays

    What gets wiped, what stays.

    After you install it, Amnesia is Off. Nothing is deleted until you tick "I understand" and press Turn On, and you can check the list first.

    Wiped at sign out

    • Browser history, cookies and logins (Chrome, Edge and others)
    • Desktop, Downloads, Documents, Pictures, Videos, Music
    • Windows traces: recent files, Run and Explorer history, searches, thumbnails, Notepad tabs, clipboard history, Recycle Bin
    • App data that is not in the vault or on the Keep List

    Stays safe

    • Everything in your Keep folder (it has a purple icon, a Desktop shortcut and a spot in Explorer's side bar)
    • Folders on your Keep List, like .ssh or your code editor's settings
    • Logins saved in the vault (back with Restore and your password)
    • Cloud folders: OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Nextcloud, MEGA, Box, pCloud, Proton Drive and every other folder Windows knows as a sync folder
    • Virtual machines (VirtualBox, Hyper-V, VMware, WSL, Docker, Android emulator) and disc images of 1 GB or more
    • Microsoft Store app data and saved app passwords, unless you switch them on in Settings
    • Your installed programs, other drives and what Windows needs to sign you in

    05 · Made for a real PC

    Wipes what it should. Leaves what it shouldn't.

    Version 0.8 came from a full audit and a test on a real Windows 11 laptop. These are the rules it follows at every sign out.

    • Cleans the moment Windows asks. So a normal sign out is clean before the sign in screen shows. Files Windows still holds open go at the next sign in, and the safe box closes after the cleanup.
    • Cloud folders stay. Moving them away would look like "deleted" to the sync app, and it could delete your files in the cloud too. Files that only live in the cloud are left alone as well.
    • Virtual machines stay. VM disks and disc images of 1 GB or more are kept. You can switch this off in Settings, Protection.
    • New apps wait 3 days. With "Remove new apps" on, a new app's folder goes to Amnesia's trash first, never straight to nothing. Press Keep and its program and data folders go on your Keep List.

    Honest trade-off: because the cleanup starts when Windows asks, cancelling the sign out after that point doesn't bring anything back. It's like pressing "Clean now".

    06 · Profile Vault

    One password. Your logins back.

    An encrypted safe for your app logins: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, Session, Element, Obsidian, Thunderbird, Bitwarden, VS Code, Claude, Antigravity, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Git and any folder you add. Sign in to a clean PC, type one password, and they're back.

    • AES-256. Every snapshot gets its own random key, locked with the vault's key. Forget the password and nobody can open it.
    • Saved after you close each app. In the background, only when something changed. Sign out just cleans, so it's quick.
    • A spare snapshot. Each app keeps the one before the newest, in case the newest looks wrong.
    • Finds your apps. It suggests installed apps it doesn't know yet, and a meter shows how hard your password is to guess.

    Memory Recall Restore brings your logins back with your password. A notification after sign in reminds you.

    Global Amnesia 3 wrong passwords or your panic word, and the vault is gone for good.

    Honest note: Windows ties saved logins to your Windows user. Snapshots come back on this PC and this user, not on another PC.

    08 · Backup

    Backup, and a new PC.

    Your Keep folder, Keep List and vault in one locked file. It's locked with your vault, so no extra password.

    • To a USB drive, another drive, a cloud folder (OneDrive, Google Drive for desktop) or your own server over SSH. Every day or every week.
    • Every backup is a new dated file. Amnesia never deletes old ones.
    • Files that were in use are named in the result, so you know what to back up again. A restore cut off by a power cut cleans up after itself and puts your old vault back.

    Memory Recall Move to a new PC: install Amnesia, pick a backup, type your vault password. Your Keep folder, Keep List and vault come back.

    09 · Quick exits

    Need to go? It cleans up.

    For nosy people, a borrowed PC, or someone pushing you to "just open it". All of these are off until you turn them on in Settings.

    Panic hotkey

    Press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + X (or Q, or F12). The screen locks at once, Amnesia cleans up and signs you out without waiting for other apps. It works while paused too, and tries again if Windows is slow.

    CtrlAltShiftX

    USB key

    Pick a USB drive. Pull it out and Amnesia cleans and signs out, or cleans and shuts down. Your choice.

    Sign out when idle

    Walked away? After 5 to 60 minutes you get a 60 second countdown, then it locks, cleans and signs out. Move the mouse to stop it.

    Panic word

    Asked for the vault password? Type your panic word instead. The vault is deleted on the spot, and your Keep folder too if you chose so.

    Doomsday

    3 wrong vault passwords in a row delete the vault for good. Amnesia warns you before the last try. You can switch it off.

    Honest fine print: Amnesia protects you after a sign out. While you're signed in, your PC is as open as always, so lock it (Win+L). Quick exits don't wait for unsaved work, and the panic word can't be undone.

    10 · Locks

    Lock it down, honestly.

    Two locks in Settings, Advanced, and a safe box in your Keep List page. Here's what they do, and what they don't.

    Keep lock

    Your Keep folder only opens through Amnesia, with its own password: Open Keep, the tray menu or the Desktop shortcut. It locks again when you lock the screen (Win+L), after the minutes you pick, and at sign out and sign in. While locked, no program can open or write files in it.

    • The files are not encrypted. Use BitLocker for that.
    • File names stay visible while it's locked.
    • Someone signed in as you who knows Windows can remove the lock in the folder's Properties.

    Lab lock

    For a PC many people use, like a computer lab or a library. An administrator sets a PIN of 6 or more digits. From then on this Windows user can't turn Amnesia off, pause it, quit it, uninstall it or change its settings and Keep List. Amnesia starts at every sign in, and the PIN opens the settings for 10 minutes.

    • Someone can still end Amnesia in Task Manager. The check at the next sign in cleans anyway.
    • Set your settings and Keep List first. They're locked as they are.

    Safe box experimental

    A locked folder inside Keep for your most private files, with its own password. Closed, it is one encrypted file (AES-256-GCM, the same format as the vault). Open, it is Keep\Safe. Amnesia closes it again at every sign out, after the cleanup.

    • Keep it small: closing packs everything again, and that takes time.
    • Forget its password and nobody can open it, you included.
    • Uninstall warns you while it still has files in it.

    11 · Health check

    Is it working? Just look.

    One page answers "can Amnesia do its job right now?". Green is fine, yellow and red tell you what to look at, and each line says where to fix it.

    • What it checks. On or off, starting with Windows, the last cleanup, files left for the next sign in, Windows Security blocks, cloud folders, virtual machines, the Keep lock, the vault, your last backup, the panic hotkey, updates, free disk space, memory and battery.
    • Startup switched off? If someone turns Amnesia off in Task Manager, Home shows a warning with a "Switch it back on" button.
    • Signed updates. Updates install only when the developer's signature on SHA256SUMS.txt checks out. Without it, Amnesia opens the release page instead of installing anything.

    What Amnesia can't do, in the app itself

    • A wipe deletes files, it doesn't overwrite the disk. Turn on device encryption.
    • Windows keeps a few traces outside your user folder.
    • The vault and backups are as strong as your password.
    • No paid code signing certificate yet, so SmartScreen can warn.

    12 · What gets wiped

    Look first. Then decide.

    "What gets wiped" is a dry run of your next sign out. It lists every file, folder and trace that would go. Opening it deletes nothing.

    • Search. Type a name or a path, like "Downloads" or ".pdf".
    • One folder. Pick a folder, with how many items and how big.
    • Biggest first. See what takes the most space.

    Review & sign out shows the same list right before you sign out, plus the programs that would be removed, with a button to keep something first.

    13 · Private

    Nothing leaves your PC.

    Amnesia has no servers, no accounts, no tracking and no analytics. The update check only asks GitHub for the newest version number.

    No servers No accounts No tracking AES-256 No admin to install No hidden PowerShell Panic word

    More, when you want it.

    Everything below is in Settings. The extras are off until you turn them on.

    Stop Windows remembering

    Windows' own privacy switches, just for you: no recent lists, no clipboard history, no web results in Start, no ad ID, Recall and Copilot off. Undo all puts back exactly what was there.

    Remove new apps

    Programs installed just for you after Turn On are removed at sign out, Microsoft Store apps too. Their folders wait 3 days in Amnesia's trash first. Press Keep and the app's program and data folders go on your Keep List.

    Keep an app

    Pick an installed app from a list and Amnesia keeps every folder it saves its data in, logins and settings included.

    Store data and saved passwords

    Two extra switches: also wipe Microsoft Store app data, and also wipe passwords apps saved in Credential Manager. Never your Microsoft account, OneDrive or Office.

    Find leftovers

    Folders and registry settings that uninstalled programs left behind. Nothing goes until you tick it.

    Throwaway Windows

    Opens Windows Sandbox for a strange file or website. Gone when you close it. Needs Windows Pro or Enterprise.

    Windows protections

    For the whole PC (admin, asked once): empty the page file at shut down, hibernation off, Recall and activity history off.

    Signed updates

    The update check reads the developer's signature on the release. No valid signature, no install: it opens the release page instead.

    Extra cleaning list

    The community list Winapp2 (about 4,000 apps), downloaded from GitHub when you press the button. Saved passwords are never touched.

    Antivirus friendly

    The cleanup runs inside Amnesia.exe. If Windows Security blocks something, Home tells you how to allow Amnesia. Never "turn your antivirus off".

    History

    When the PC was cleaned, which apps were saved, when backups ran. Never file names.

    Collect log

    Something not working? One text file in your Keep folder, without file names. Nothing is sent. You choose what to share.

    Disk encryption check

    Settings shows whether BitLocker or device encryption is on, and how to turn it on.

    Wipe free space

    Runs Windows' own cipher /w, so deleted files are harder to bring back. Slow, and it asks first.

    Care. Space back, with a list first.

    Amnesia already wipes most of your user folder at every sign out, so Care is for what stays: your Keep folder, the apps you kept and big files from this session. Every action scans first and shows a list, and nothing risky is ticked for you.

    Disk overview

    See what takes space: Keep folder, vault, Recycle Bin, Temp, the biggest folders and files, old downloads. Read only, nothing is deleted here.

    Clean now

    Empties Temp, crash dumps, app caches and Amnesia's trash. Files Windows still has open are skipped. The Recycle Bin is listed, but never ticked for you.

    Uninstall apps

    Programs installed just for you run their own uninstaller. Programs for everyone open Windows Settings. Leftover caches go to the Recycle Bin, personal data stays unless you tick it.

    Project build folders

    node_modules, target, build, dist, and bin and obj of .NET projects can go to the Recycle Bin. Folders used in the last 7 days or tracked by git are left alone.

    Old installers

    Old .msi and .msix files go to the Recycle Bin. Setup .exe files are listed, but never picked for you, and neither are disk images or virtual machines.

    Safe tune-ups

    Restart Explorer, or open Startup apps and Storage settings. No admin needed.

    Stops when unsure

    If the Keep List can't be read or has gone missing, the cleanup deletes nothing and says so in History. When a check can't tell, the item is kept.

    Every run in History

    Every Care run is in History with the space freed. The Health check also shows free disk space, memory and battery.

    Modes and profiles. You choose how much is forgotten.

    Pick one mode for every sign out, restore only the apps you need, and ask an AI for advice without sharing your files. Every change shows a preview first and takes effect at the next sign out.

    Retrograde Amnesia

    Like a brand-new account at every sign in. Only your Keep folder and what Amnesia needs to work stay. The vault's auto snapshot at sign out is turned off, and you can still restore a snapshot by hand.

    Selective Amnesia (recommended)

    Your Keep folder stays and app logins come back from the vault. The same as before.

    Partial Amnesia

    Everything in AppData stays, so apps keep their settings and logins, and their caches too. Your files, Downloads and Desktop are wiped. Browsing history and cookies inside a browser's own folder stay too, because that folder is kept.

    Custom Amnesia

    Your own Keep List. Picked for you when you change the lines a mode added. A mode only changes its own marked section, so your own lines always stay.

    A preview before every switch

    See what will now be wiped and what will now be kept, with a warning when more is wiped. Nothing is deleted when you switch. A missing or damaged mode list never empties your Keep List.

    Vault profiles

    Restore only what you need, like Work or Personal, or everything. Rename, add and remove profiles. One browser holds one set of logins, so a second browser keeps personal use apart.

    Ask an AI

    Amnesia makes a report with no file contents and no names of your files, plus a ready prompt. Paste it into the AI you choose, paste the answer back and tick the changes you want. Amnesia itself sends nothing.

      Tick "I understand" on the home card, then press Turn On. This session stays as it is until you sign out.

      • Selective Amnesia from now on, every sign out, restart and shut down forgets everything except what you chose.
      • Health check shows green when everything is ready.

      07 / 08

      Sign out, then restore

      Review & sign out shows what goes, then signs you out. After you sign in again, open Vault, type the password and press Restore.

      • Memory Recall your apps come back signed in.
      • Cancelling a sign out after Windows asked doesn't undo the cleanup.

      08 / 08

      Updating Amnesia

      Always in this order: Turn Off, install the update, Turn On. Settings → Update → Check now does it for you.

      • It only installs a release with the developer's valid signature. Otherwise it opens the release page.

        Nothing is deleted before step 7.

        17 · FAQ

        Questions, answered.

        Safety

        Can I try it without deleting anything?

        Yes. After you install it, Amnesia is Off and deletes nothing. Open What gets wiped and press Show preview: it only lists what would go. Turn it on when the list looks right.

        Where do wiped files go? Can I get them back?

        Nowhere you can reach. They don't go to the Recycle Bin and can't be brought back. Once Amnesia is on, it really deletes, so check What gets wiped first and put what you need in Keep or on the Keep List.

        Is my Keep folder encrypted?

        No. Amnesia keeps it, it doesn't encrypt it. Turn on BitLocker or device encryption: Settings → Privacy & security → Device encryption. Amnesia's Settings shows whether it's on.

        What if the PC crashes or loses power?

        The check at the next sign in catches it and does the cleanup.

        What if I cancel a sign out?

        Amnesia cleans the moment Windows asks "may I sign out?". If you cancel after that, for example because an app asked to save, the cleanup has already run. It's like pressing "Clean now". Use Review & sign out when you're not sure.

        Are OneDrive, Dropbox or my virtual machines wiped?

        No. Cloud folders (OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Nextcloud, MEGA, Box, pCloud, Proton Drive and any Windows sync folder) are left alone, because moving them could delete your files in the cloud too. VM disks and disc images of 1 GB or more stay as well, unless you switch that off in Settings, Protection.

        How do I know it's working?

        Open Health check on the home page. Each line is green, yellow or red and says where to fix it. History shows every cleanup with how many items went.

        Is my data sent anywhere?

        No. No servers, no accounts, no tracking. Backups go only where you pick. The update check asks GitHub for the newest version number (every week by default), and you can switch it off.

        Windows Security or my antivirus blocks it. Now what?

        Home tells you when something was refused. Usually it's Controlled folder access: Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Ransomware protection → Allow an app, and add Amnesia. Please don't turn your antivirus off.

        Locks and quick exits

        Can someone get around the Keep lock?

        Yes, someone signed in as you who knows Windows can remove it in the folder's Properties. The files aren't encrypted either. It stops casual snooping and other programs, not a determined person. For real protection, use BitLocker.

        I forgot my Keep lock password.

        Open the Keep folder's Properties → Security and remove the "Deny" lines. Your files are still there.

        What happens with the panic hotkey?

        The screen locks right away, Amnesia cleans up and signs you out without waiting for other apps. Unsaved work is lost. The tray menu has the same thing: Panic sign out now.

        What is the safe box?

        An experimental locked folder inside Keep, for your most private files. Closed, it's one encrypted file (AES-256-GCM, like the vault). Open, it's Keep\Safe. Amnesia closes it again at every sign out, after the cleanup. Keep it small: closing packs everything again.

        Can the lab lock be stopped?

        Someone can end Amnesia in Task Manager. The check at the next sign in still cleans. Changing settings or turning it off needs the administrator's PIN.

        Profile Vault

        What if I forget the vault password?

        Then nobody can open it, including you. Make a new vault and sign in to your apps again. Write the password down somewhere safe.

        When is the vault saved?

        After you close an app that changed, in the background (checked every minute). Tools without a window, like Claude Code or Git, after 3 minutes without changes. If Edge keeps running in the background ("startup boost"), it's saved once it really closes.

        Do my logins work on another PC?

        Usually not. Windows ties saved logins to your Windows user, so snapshots come back on this PC and this user. Move to a new PC brings your Keep folder, Keep List and vault, but you may need to sign in to some apps again.

        The newest snapshot looks wrong.

        If a new snapshot is much smaller than the last one, the vault says so. Tick "Use the snapshot before the newest one" when you restore.

        What are the panic word and Doomsday?

        The panic word is an optional word you pick. Typed in the password box, it deletes the vault (and your Keep folder too, if you chose so). Doomsday deletes the vault after 3 wrong passwords in a row. You can switch Doomsday off.

        Install and updates

        Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

        The app isn't signed yet, so Windows doesn't know it. Press More info, then Run anyway. Want to be sure? Compare the file with SHA256SUMS.txt from the same release.

        x64 or arm64?

        Most PCs need x64. Pick arm64 for Windows on ARM, like a Snapdragon laptop or a Windows virtual machine on a Mac.

        Does it need admin?

        No. It installs just for you. Only a few optional things ask Windows for admin once: Windows protections, deleting leftover folders and the lab lock.

        How do I update?

        Settings → Update → Check now. Turn Amnesia off before you install the update, then turn it on again. Amnesia installs an update only when the developer's signature checks out; otherwise it opens the release page.

        How do I uninstall it?

        Settings → Uninstall Amnesia, or Apps & features like any app. It turns off first, so nothing more is wiped. Your Keep folder always stays. If Keep is locked it asks for the Keep password, and it warns you if the safe box still has files. To delete settings, Keep List and vault too, type DELETE in the box.

        Do Microsoft Store apps and new programs get removed?

        Only with "Remove new apps" on. Then a program installed just for you after Turn On is removed at sign out, and its folder waits 3 days in Amnesia's trash first. Press Keep to keep it. Store apps keep their own data unless you switch on "Also wipe Store app data".

        Is there a Mac version?

        Yes, and it's been around longer. See the Mac page.

        Found a bug? Want something changed?

        Open an issue on GitHub. Please don't paste passwords, private file lists or anything personal. Collect log leaves file names out for that reason.
