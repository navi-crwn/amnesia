# Terms of use & warning

Please read this before you install Amnesia or turn it on. It's short, promise.

## ⚠️ Amnesia deletes data. For real.

That's the whole point of the app. Once you press **Turn On**:

- Everything outside your **Keep folder** and your **Keep List** is deleted at every logout, restart and shutdown, then checked again at the next login.
- That includes your Desktop, Downloads, Documents, Pictures, browser history, cookies, app data and saved passwords in the Keychain (except the ones on the Keep List).
- Deleted files **don't go to the Trash** and **can't be brought back**. Not by you, not by us.

Amnesia stays **off** until you turn it on yourself. Before you do:

1. Back up anything you care about.
2. Open Settings → **See what would be deleted** and read the list.
3. Move important files into your Keep folder.

## 🔐 Your passwords are yours alone

- The **Profile Vault** and **backups** are locked with passwords only you know.
- If you forget the vault or backup password, **nobody can open it**. There's no reset and no back door.
- Typing the wrong vault password 3 times, or typing your panic word, **destroys the vault on purpose**.

## 🏠 Your data stays on your Mac

- Amnesia has **no servers**, **no accounts**, **no tracking** and **no analytics**. It doesn't collect anything.
- Nothing leaves your Mac unless **you** set up a backup to a server or cloud. Even then, only the encrypted `.7z` file is sent, straight from your Mac to the place you picked.
- Cloud logins (Google Drive, Dropbox…) go through rclone and are stored on your Mac only.

By installing Amnesia, or by ticking "I've read this" in the app, you agree to all of the above.
