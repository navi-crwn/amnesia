BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Amnesia is an app that forgets on purpose. After you turn it on, your user folder is cleaned at every sign out, except your Keep folder and your Keep List.

Installing does NOT turn it on. Nothing is deleted until you press Turn On in the app yourself.

Good to know:
  - Install it in your own Windows account only. A spare test account is a good first step.
  - If Amnesia was already on and you are updating: turn it Off first, install, then turn it On again.
  - Make a backup of anything important before you turn it on.
